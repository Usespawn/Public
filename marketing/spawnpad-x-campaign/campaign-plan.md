# SpawnPad — X Campaign Plan

## Objectives
1. Introduce SpawnPad as a serious Solana launchpad with a signature feature: vanity mints ending in exact `Spw`.
2. Educate the audience on how the feature actually works (and what it is not).
3. Build a follow base before the first controlled launch and the official CA reveal.
4. Establish a transparent, engineering-first brand voice.

## Target audience
- Solana token creators who want their launch to be recognizable.
- Pump.fun users who already understand bonding-curve launches.
- Crypto-native builders and designers who follow "building in public" accounts.

## Core messaging
- **Tagline:** "Every token has an origin. Make yours SpawnPad."
- **Pillar 1 — Identity:** the mint address itself carries the mark (`Spw`).
- **Pillar 2 — Honesty:** real cryptographic generation, exact validation, on-chain verification; no fake metrics, no promises of price or profit.
- **Pillar 3 — Craft:** a guided 7-step flow from idea to on-chain.

## Posting sequence (recommended order)
Post the numbers as numbered — the narrative was written to unfold in this
order, but **every tweet also stands alone** if seen out of order:

1. `01-introducing-spawnpad` — flagship introduction
2. `02-why-spawnpad` — the philosophy
3. `03-vanity-mint-spw` — the signature feature
4. `04-how-vanity-works` — how it works (credibility)
5. `05-pumpfun-integration` — the engine (+ not-affiliated note)
6. `06-user-benefits` — concrete capabilities
7. `07-launch-flow` — the how-to
8. `08-built-for-solana` — the network stance
9. `09-token-identity` — branding angle
10. `10-under-the-hood` — engineering credibility
11. `11-launch-transparency` — safety culture
12. `12-whats-next` — roadmap (plans only)
13. `13-building-in-public` — proof of work
14. `14-community-invitation` — the invite
15. `15-campaign-closer` — the vision

## Cadence
**Recommendation: one post per day** (15 days ≈ three weeks of presence).
Keep the daily slot consistent (e.g., 14:00–16:00 UTC). This is a
recommendation, not a requirement — batches of 2–3 per day also work if a
launch announcement needs the runway cleared.

## Engaging with replies
- Reply within the first hour of each post while the algorithm is weighting it.
- Answer technical questions with specifics (link the docs page for depth).
- Never promise price outcomes, listings, or returns — redirect to the product.
- Credit anyone who reports bugs; "fix shipped" replies build trust.
- Pin the tweet that performs best (likely 01, 03, or 07).

## Sharing product screenshots & demos
- Use real UI screenshots only (`/launch`, `/explore`) — no mocked numbers.
- Short screen recordings of the vanity grinder (attempts counter climbing to a
  `Spw` match) are the most distinctive demo asset.
- Always overlay the demonstration-address label when showing an address.

## Metrics to evaluate
- **Impressions** per post (identify which pillars resonate).
- **Engagement rate** (replies + reposts + likes ÷ impressions).
- **Profile visits** and **follows per post** (via X analytics).
- **Link clicks** (to x.com/spawnpadHQ bio link and github.com/Usespawn).
- Quality signal: ratio of technical questions to generic replies.
- Review at day 7 and day 15; let the best-performing pillars shape future posts.

## What NOT to do (guardrails)
- No engagement farming, giveaway bait, or fake scarcity.
- No price talk, no "guaranteed" anything.
- No posting as if Pump.fun endorsed SpawnPad.
- Never present the demonstration address as an official token CA.
